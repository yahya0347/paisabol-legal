Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 September 2026 · Published by Muhammad Yahya Aslam · Contact: m.yahyaaslamofficial@gmail.com

This policy explains what PaisaBol does with your information. It is written to be read, not to be impressive. Where something leaves your phone, we say so.

The short version
  • Your money data — transactions, budgets, goals — is stored on your phone, encrypted. We have no server, so we never receive it.
  • Voice is different. When you speak to PaisaBol, the audio is sent to your phone's speech service (on most Android phones, Google's) to be turned into text. That audio is handled by Google, not by us.
  • If you would rather no audio left your phone, type your transactions instead. Everything in PaisaBol can be entered by typing.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's Advertising ID.
  • There is no password recovery and no sign-in on a second device.
  • Uninstalling PaisaBol deletes your data.

1. Who we are

PaisaBol is published by Muhammad Yahya Aslam. You can reach us at m.yahyaaslamofficial@gmail.com. PaisaBol is a personal finance app for Android, available to users worldwide.

2. We do not run a server

PaisaBol has no backend, no user database and no cloud account. Our own code makes no network requests. There is no PaisaBol server for your financial data to be sent to, and no account of yours exists anywhere except on your phone.

Two pieces of software bundled in the app do talk to Google: the Google AdMob advertising SDK, and your phone's speech recognition service. Both are described below. We mention this plainly because saying "PaisaBol sends no data" would be misleading. PaisaBol itself sends none. Those two Google components do.

We do not sell your financial information, and we do not use your financial records for our own marketing. There is no mechanism by which we could: your transactions, udhar records, budgets and goals are encrypted on your own device with a key we do not hold, and the app sends none of it anywhere. We have no copy to sell, share or advertise against.

3. Your money data stays on your phone

Stored only in PaisaBol's own storage on your device:

  • transactions — amount, date, category, merchant name, and any note
  • budgets and savings goals
  • your categories, including ones you create
  • your profile — first, middle and last name, an optional short bio, email address, optional photo, the country you selected, your chosen currency, theme and notification settings
  • your password and app-lock PIN, stored only as one-way hashes (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 210,000 iterations, with a random salt). The app cannot recover the originals
  • corrections you make to categories or merchants, so the app guesses better next time. You can clear these at any time from Profile → Privacy & data

This data is encrypted on disk using AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is generated on your device and held by the Android Keystore. It is never included in a backup, never sent anywhere, and never written into our logs. We never receive any of it, and could not hand it to anyone if asked, because we do not have it.

What the PIN does, and does not do

Your PIN and fingerprint/face unlock control access to the app's screens. Encryption of the file itself is protected by the key in the Android Keystore. These are two separate mechanisms. A four-digit PIN has only ten thousand possible values, so we do not use it as an encryption key and do not claim it as one.

4. Voice — audio leaves your phone

This is the most important thing in this policy. When you hold the PaisaBol bubble and speak, your audio is sent to your phone's speech recognition service — on most Android phones, Google's — to be turned into text.

We have confirmed this on a real device. Whether a given phone transcribes on-device or over the network is decided by that phone's speech service and its installed language packs — not by PaisaBol. On the device we tested, the offline language pack was not installed and the online recogniser was used.

  • The audio is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, not ours
  • PaisaBol receives only the text that comes back
  • We do not record, store or upload audio ourselves. PaisaBol keeps no audio files at any point
  • The text is saved with the transaction it created, on your phone, inside the encrypted storage described above

If you do not want your audio leaving your phone, do not use the voice feature. Every transaction can be entered by typing instead — use "Add Expense" or "Add Income", or "Use text instead" on the voice screen. Typing sends no audio anywhere. We are not able to force your phone to transcribe offline, so we do not offer a setting that pretends to.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PaisaBol is free and funded by ads. There is no subscription, no paid tier and no way to pay to remove ads.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device's Advertising ID — a resettable identifier Android provides for advertising — and connects to Google's servers to fetch ads. That data is collected by Google under Google's privacy policy.

  • We do not send your transactions, amounts, merchants, notes, budgets, goals, name, email or photo to AdMob or to any advertiser. Ads have no access to your financial data
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads

Ads appear on the Home, Transactions, Calendar, Insights, Budgets, Goals and Profile screens. Ads never appear on the sign-in, sign-up, PIN lock, onboarding or welcome screens, and never during voice input.

6. Camera and photos

If you choose to set a profile photo, PaisaBol asks for camera or photo access at that moment. The photo is stored inside your encrypted data on this phone and is never uploaded. If you never set a photo, the app never uses these permissions.

7. Exporting your data

You can export from Settings → Data → Export as PDF or CSV.

Restore. Settings → Data → Restore reads a JSON backup file. JSON is no longer offered as an export option, so this is for a backup made by an earlier version of the app, or one you produced yourself. Restoring replaces what is currently in the app. The file is read on your device and is not uploaded anywhere.

Exported files are not encrypted. They are ordinary, readable files. This is deliberate: a backup has to be readable on a different device, which will not have your phone's encryption key. Once you send an export somewhere, anyone who opens it can read your financial data. Keep exports somewhere private.

Where an exported file goes is entirely your choice, made in the Android share sheet. PaisaBol does not upload it anywhere.

8. Accounts, passwords and recovery

Your PaisaBol account exists only on the phone where you created it.

  • There is no password reset email, because there is no server to send one and no way for us to verify that an address is yours
  • There is no sign-in on a second device, and no syncing between devices
  • If you forget your password or PIN, we cannot recover your data. Nobody can. The honest options are to export your data while you still have access, or erase and start again

We say this plainly so it is not a surprise later. Please export a backup while you can still get in.

9. Children

PaisaBol is not directed at children and should not be used by anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because we operate no server, we hold no such information to remove.

10. Deleting your data

  • Erase all data (Profile → Erase all data) deletes your transactions, budgets, goals and profile, and destroys the encryption key
  • Uninstalling PaisaBol removes the app's data from your phone
  • Clear learned suggestions (Profile → Privacy & data) removes only the corrections the app learned, leaving transactions untouched

Because your data never reaches us, there is nothing for you to request that we delete. Full instructions are in Deleting Your Data.

One technical caveat, stated honestly: Android may keep deleted database pages on disk until it compacts its storage files. Reinstalling PaisaBol is the only way to be certain nothing remains.

Deleting Your Data

Effective date: 5 September 2026 · Published by Muhammad Yahya Aslam · Contact: m.yahyaaslamofficial@gmail.com

Your PaisaBol data is stored only on your phone. We operate no server and hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us — deleting it is something you do on the device, and it takes a few seconds.

Deleting your data is permanent. We cannot restore it. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

Option 1 — Erase everything, keep the app installed

Open PaisaBol → Profile → Erase all data → confirm.

This deletes your transactions, budgets, goals, categories, profile and photo, and destroys the encryption key that protected them. Once the key is gone, anything left in storage cannot be decrypted by anyone.

Option 2 — Uninstall the app

Uninstalling removes the app's data from your phone: Settings → Apps → PaisaBol → Uninstall, or press and hold the app icon and choose Uninstall. Because PaisaBol has no server, uninstalling leaves nothing behind anywhere else.

Option 3 — Clear only what the app learned

Profile → Privacy & data → Clear learned suggestions. Your transactions, budgets and goals are not affected.

Deleting specific things

To deleteWhere
One transactionTransactions → tap it → Delete
A budget or goalBudgets / Goals → the delete icon on that item
A custom categoryProfile → Categories → swipe the category left → Delete
Your profile photoProfile → tap your photo → Remove
Learned suggestionsProfile → Privacy & data → Clear learned suggestions

Deleting a custom category does not delete the transactions filed under it. Those are moved to "Other" so your spending history stays intact, and the app tells you before you confirm.

Export before you delete

Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered — by you or by us. To keep a copy: Settings → Data → Export, then choose PDF or CSV.

  • Exported files are not encrypted. Anyone who opens the file can read your financial data. Keep it somewhere private
  • PDF and CSV are for reading and for spreadsheets. Neither can be imported back into the app, so an export is a copy to keep — not a way to move your data to a new phone

What about data that is not on your phone?

Two things happen outside PaisaBol, and neither is ours to delete.

Voice audio. When you use voice input, your audio is sent to your phone's speech recognition service — on most Android phones, Google's — to be turned into text. PaisaBol keeps no audio. Any retention is Google's, under Google's privacy policy. You can manage that data in your Google Account: myactivity.google.com. If you would rather no audio ever left your phone, enter transactions by typing instead of speaking.

Advertising ID. Google AdMob uses your device's Advertising ID to show ads. You can reset or delete it yourself in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

One honest technical note

When Android deletes data from its storage files, the old bytes can remain on disk until the system compacts those files. This is how Android's storage works, not something the app can force. If you need certainty that nothing recoverable is left, uninstall and reinstall PaisaBol, or use your phone's factory reset.
